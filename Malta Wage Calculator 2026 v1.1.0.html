❓ How to use this app

Everything explained in plain words 👇
🚀 1. Quick start
Three steps:
  • 1️⃣ Type your pay in the big box (before tax — the number on your contract).
  • 2️⃣ Tap per hour / week / month / year so the app knows what kind of number it is.
  • 3️⃣ Set your hours per week — done! Your green take-home number appears. 💚
The big green number follows the way you typed your pay — typed per month? You see per month 📅. The other views sit in small cards, plus a card with your pay BEFORE tax so you can double-check. 👀 Everything you type is saved on your phone automatically. 🔒 Nothing is sent anywhere.
💶 2. Ways to enter your pay
You can enter your wage any way you know it:
  • Per hour — e.g. 10 with 40 hours/week.
  • Per week — e.g. 450.
  • Per month — e.g. 1950 (app turns it into yearly ÷ 12).
  • Per year — e.g. 23400.
The big green result matches the chip you picked: per month shows what lands in your pocket at the end of the month 📅. Week & year sit in the small cards next to it, so you can compare with friends easily. 🤝 The full breakdown table shows week · month · year side by side — including exactly how much tax and SSC came out. 🧾
⏰ 3. Overtime & the special 15% rule
Add your extra hours:
  • 1.5× — normal extra hours (over 40/week) on weekdays & Saturdays.
  • 2× — Sundays and public holidays (double pay).
⚡ Special rule: if your basic pay is €375/week or less and you're not a manager, your first €10,000 of overtime per year is taxed at only 15%. Switch it on in "Your tax setup" — the app tells you if you qualify. ✅
👪 4. Tax status & the NEW 2026 family tables
Malta has different tax ladders. Pick yours:
  • 🧍 Single — tax-free up to €12,000.
  • 💑 Married (joint) — tax-free up to €15,000.
  • 👩‍👧 Parent — tax-free up to €13,000.
  • 🆕 NEW in Budget 2026: special bigger tables for married or parent WITH children — tax-free up to €22,500 (married, 2+ kids)! Try them and watch the tax shrink. 🪄
The children's tables grow again in 2027 and 2028 — this app uses the exact 2026 ones.
🏛️ 5. What gets taken out of your wage
Only two things in Malta:
  • 🏛️ SSC / NI — 10% of your basic weekly wage (pension, health, benefits). Capped at €55.93/week in 2026 if born 1962 or later (€49.04 if older). Big earners never pay more than that. 💡
  • 🧾 Income tax — 0% / 15% / 25% / 35% in steps. High tax only on the high slices, never on everything.
That's it — no city tax, no hidden payroll taxes. 🙌
🎁 6. Bonus, allowance & COLA
  • 🎁 Statutory bonus & weekly allowance — €512.52/year total, every full-timer (paid in 4 parts: March, June, September, December). Switch it off if you're part-time or started mid-year.
  • 📈 COLA 2026 — everyone's wage went up €4.66/week this January. It's already inside your wage — no need to add it.
📏 7. Minimum wage checker
Under your green number there's a badge:
  • ✅ green — you're at or above the 2026 minimum.
  • ⚠️ red — the hourly rate you typed is BELOW the legal minimum (2026: €229.44/wk · €5.74/h age 18+, €222.66 age 17, €219.82 under 17). If that badge is red, check your contract with the DIER! 📞
Pick your age in "Your tax setup" so the check is right for you.
⚠️ 8. Honest small print
This app gives very close estimates using the official 2026 public tables (CFR / DIER / Social Security). Real payslips can differ because of:
  • 🕒 part-time or mid-year starts (bonus & SSC are pro-rata),
  • 📄 your sector's WRO rules (some have higher minimums, e.g. security),
  • 🧮 rounding to the cent on real payrolls,
  • 💼 special cases (second jobs, self-employed, student schemes).
For the final word always check your payslip or cfr.gov.mt. 🤝 Runs fully offline — your numbers never leave your phone. 📴
