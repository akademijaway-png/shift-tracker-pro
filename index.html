❓ How to Use — Quick Guide

Tap a topic to open it 👇

⏱️ Start & stop a shift

Home → big blue START button — the clock ticks and counts your € live. Tap it again (FINISH) when work ends. Forgot to press start? My Earnings → ＋ Add Shift.Every finished shift lands in 📜 History — a long list opens instantly (newest 40); tap ⬇️ Show older shifts at the bottom when you need more (keeps the app snappy ⚡).

🔁 Auto shifts (set once, runs alone)

Settings → 🔁 Add Shift (auto) → pick your days → tap a quick time (like ☀️ 07–19) → Save. The clock starts & stops by itself on those days. 🔵 blue dot on the calendar = auto shift planned. Tap the day → ✏️ change or 🗑️ remove just that day. Repeat choices: every week, this week only, 📅 next week only (fills next Mon–Sun, then stops — perfect when the new roster just landed), or until the end of the month. 💶 Different wage on different days? In that same form, wage for each day (optional) — type a €/h next to each selected day that pays differently; empty days keep your normal rate, and everything is calculated with it automatically (payslips, payday, live counter). Break not paid? Tick 🍔 Unpaid break in that same form and set the minutes.

🗓️ Calendar

‹ › = previous / next month, « » = jump a whole year. ★ = Maltese public holiday (special pay 💶). Tap any day to see its shifts, notes and reminders.

💶 Wage & pay rules

Settings → 💶 My Hourly Wage: set your €/hour and tick what your workplace pays (Sunday ×2, holidays, rest days, overtime). Pay is then counted automatically on every shift. Home → 🧮 Pay Calc = quick day-rate what-if maths. 🍔 Unpaid break? Tick it in any shift form (add / edit / auto) or right here in Pay Calc — those minutes come straight off the paid time, e.g. 12h − 30m = 11.5h paid. Same break every day? Settings → 💶 My Hourly Wage → tick 🍔 Unpaid break and set the minutes once — every NEW shift (⏱️ Start/Finish too, plus auto rules) starts with it ticked; you can still change or untick it per shift. 🗓️ Payday tracking? Settings → 🗓️ Payday → tick Enable → pick your payday day (or set the exact next date). The dashboard then shows the calendar month AND the payday period (like 15 Aug → 14 Sep), and 📜 Salary History keeps every finished period with its full maths — the normal monthly payslip stays exactly as it was.

💚 Take-Home Calc 🇲🇹 2026 (NEW): Home → My Earnings → 💶 Take-Home — type your pay any way you know it (per hour / week / month / year), add ⏰ overtime (1.5× & 2×), and tick the boxes: 🎂 born 1962 or later, 🎁 statutory bonus, ⚡ overtime special 15% tax (the app checks if you qualify). Pick your 👪 tax status — all 7 official 2026 tables are inside, including the NEW family tables (married / parent with children: much less tax!). You instantly see what you REALLY keep per week / month / year after 🏛️ SSC and 🧾 tax, a 📏 minimum-wage checker (with your age!), a 🪄 Use my real week button that estimates this week's net from your actual logged shifts, and a 🏛️/🧑‍💼/🏢 job-type card (government vs agency vs private) explaining what you're entitled to. Everything is saved and syncs with your account. Numbers are close estimates from the official 2026 public tables (cfr.gov.mt · dier.gov.mt).

📄 Payslips & 📋 rosters

My Earnings → 📄 Payslip: the app BUILDS your payslip from your hours (⬇️ CSV for Excel, or 🖨️ Print → Save as PDF). My Earnings → 📎 Upload Payslip: add the payslip your employer gives you (photo or PDF) so everything sits in one safe place. My Earnings → 📋 Upload Roster: keep the roster your boss gives out (photo or PDF) — tag it like “17–30 August” and find it any time under My Roster Files.

💬 Co-workers & chat

Under your chats in 💬 Messages sits the 👥 Find Coworkers list — it shows EVERYONE registered on the app, with ＋ Add next to their name, so coworkers find each other easily (the 🔍 search by Gmail on top still works too). When someone adds you, the request waits at the TOP of Messages with Accept / Decline — the red dot counts requests too. After accepting, chat away: 📷 tap ANY photo in a chat to open it BIG, then tap anywhere (or the ✕) to close it. Messages arrive even when their app is closed.

🔔 Notifications

Settings → 🔔 Push Notifications → Enable → then 📣 Send test to try it. 🍏 iPhone: first Share → Add to Home Screen, then open the app from there.

☁️ Backup & new phone

Everything saves to your account automatically (green ☁️ Cloud Backup card). New or lost phone: install the app, sign in — your wage, shifts, pay rules, notes and payslip files come back by themselves. 📱→📲
